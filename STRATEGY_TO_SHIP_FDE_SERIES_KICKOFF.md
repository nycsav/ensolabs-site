## What we're designing
The visual identity + launch assets for **"The Forward Deployed Strategist"** — a four-part editorial series arguing that the agency/consulting strategist has always been a Forward Deployed Engineer, and AI just attached the last mile.

**Part 1 — "The best Forward Deployed Engineers have been forward deployed all along."** (the thesis / manifesto)

Series arc (for the masthead + "part x of 4" affordance):
1. The thesis — the strategist was always the engineer *(this piece)*
2. The harness is the product — deploying agents in regulated industries *(from Berkeley AAS)*
3. Write the brief before the agent — defining the outcome is what decides whether agentic deployments succeed
4. The regulated playbook — how finance actually ships agents

## Brand system — Strategy → Ship (locked; do not improvise)
- **In-body figures:** "Warm Signal" — Paper `#F7F1E6` ground, Ink `#1E1813` text, **Ship Coral `#F0512E` on ONE element only** (a single hero stat or the → arrow). Ledger Amber `#E0A23C` sparingly. Enso Teal `#5CE0D2` reserved for "from Enso Labs" links only.
- **Social/OG card:** navy/teal terminal palette — `#0d1321`-family gradient, `#5ce0d2` accent (matches the sitewide default OG template; deliberate exception for social cards).
- **Type:** Lora (headlines) · Inter Tight (body/UI) · JetBrains Mono (kickers, datelines, stat labels, "PART 1 OF 4").
- The **→ arrow glyph is always Ship Coral `#F0512E`**, never recolored.
