The managed agent

One agent. Three tools. A transparent lens.

Signal Lens is a native watsonx Orchestrate agent that orchestrates three Python tools in sequence. Every score is explainable — no black box, no padding.

STEP 01

Scout

fetch_signals()

Pulls candidate market signals for the topic within a lookback window. Today: a curated sample set; production swaps in live news, filings, and internal knowledge-base connectors.

→
STEP 02

Curate

score_signal()

Scores each signal 1–10 on four axes — relevance, materiality, urgency, confidence — and averages them. The Enso strategic lens; weights tune per client.

→
STEP 03

Publish

publish_brief()

Assembles only the 7.0+ signals into a dated, cited brief, sorted by score and led by "what changed & why it matters." Nothing clears the bar? It says so.

Reference architecture · W.L. Gore

watsonx.data is the lakehouse under the agent

Signal Lens doesn't scrape the web — it reads a governed lakehouse. Gore's internal and external data lands once in watsonx.data (open Iceberg tables, Presto engine, in-tenant), and the agent's Scout step queries it with SQL. One data foundation, every agent on top of it.
