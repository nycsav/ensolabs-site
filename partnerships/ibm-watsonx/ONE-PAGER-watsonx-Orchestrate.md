## The agent: Signal Lens

**Signal Lens is a managed research-intelligence agent** that continuously scans a defined market, scores what matters, and publishes decision-ready briefs to the people who need them. Built as a native agent on watsonx Orchestrate, it turns a noisy external landscape into a short, ranked signal each day — no analyst in the loop until a decision is actually required.

The pattern is drawn from a live Enso deployment for a **Fortune 500 manufacturer**, where a Scout → Curate → Publish engine now surfaces market and competitive signals the business previously missed. We are rebuilding that pattern natively on watsonx Orchestrate as the reference implementation for this partnership.

---

## How it works — three orchestrated steps

1. **Scout.** A retrieval tool pulls candidate signals from the sources a client cares about (news, filings, research, internal systems).
2. **Curate.** A scoring tool ranks each signal against the client's strategic lens (relevance, materiality, urgency, confidence) and drops the noise.
3. **Publish.** A publisher tool formats the top-ranked signals into a dated, cited brief and routes it to the right channel.

watsonx Orchestrate handles the reasoning, tool-calling, and multi-agent hand-offs; Enso supplies the domain scoring logic and operates the agent as a managed service.

## FAQ

**Is this a product or a service?**
Both. Signal Lens is a productized pattern ("Core"), delivered and operated by Enso as a managed agent — the client gets outcomes, not a platform to run.

**What does IBM provide vs. what does Enso provide?**
IBM provides the watsonx Orchestrate runtime, models, governance, and distribution. Enso provides the domain scoring logic, the deployment, and ongoing operation.

**How fast can a client see value?**
The Scout → Curate → Publish loop produces a first ranked brief on day one of a deployment; tuning the scoring lens to the client's strategy takes the first working sessions.

**Which industries fit first?**
Manufacturing and financial services — buyers with a large external signal surface and a low tolerance for missing a material move.
